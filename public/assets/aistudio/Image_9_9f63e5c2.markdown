## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an open, community-driven social commerce marketplace. The experience centers around peer-to-peer trust, rapid catalog scanning, and direct communication.

### Brand Personality & Mood
- **Trustworthy & Dependable:** Familiar, secure interface cues that eliminate friction in high-stakes financial and in-person transactions.
- **Vibrant & Accessible:** High-energy operational blues balanced with neutral utility surfaces so user-generated media and product listings remain the focal point.
- **Utility-First & Snappy:** Instant affordance recognition, crisp data density, and zero-latency layout clarity.

### Visual Style
- **Corporate / Modern Consumer Utility:** Inspired by contemporary social-graph interfaces, employing subtle gray containment surfaces (`#F0F2F5`), pristine card modules (`#FFFFFF`), light micro-borders, and tactile 12px–16px radiuses.

## Colors

The palette balances signature social-app blues with utility grays designed to make user-uploaded photography pop.

### Color Roles
- **Primary (`#0866FF`) & Active Blue (`#1877F2`):** Primary action buttons, active navigation states, notification badges, verified seller checkmarks, and focus indicators.
- **Page Background (`#F0F2F5`):** The master canvas background that frames card content and provides clear surface separation.
- **Card & Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white surfaces for cards, modals, dropdown menus, and search bars.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Subtle divider boundaries (`#E4E6EB`) and structural element borders (`#CED0D4`).
- **Typography Tiers:** Primary charcoal text (`#050505`) ensures AA/AAA contrast ratios; secondary muted metadata (`#65676B`) supports timestamps, seller metrics, and product category subtitles.
- **Functional Semantics:** Success green (`#31A24C`) for listing availability and pricing drops; Destructive red (`#FA383E`) for reporting, cancellations, and alerts.

## Typography

The type scale uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to combine high screen legibility with approachable, contemporary geometry.

- **Listing Pricing:** Uses `headline-md` or `headline-sm` with `fontWeight: 700` for clear visual scanning across search grids.
- **Seller Details & Timestamps:** Uses `body-sm` set to `#65676B` to maintain hierarchy without cluttering listing cards.
- **Action Buttons & Chips:** Utilize `label-md` and `label-lg` with tight vertical metrics to guarantee vertical centering inside compact UI controls.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture engineered for responsive, high-density listing grids and multi-pane desktop navigation.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Global Structure:** A fixed left-hand rail (360px) for categories and localized filters on desktop, transitioning into a multi-column fluid product grid.
- **Product Grids:**
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 2-column grid layout with `gutter: 0.75rem` and `margin: 1rem`.
  - **Tablet (640px–1024px):** 3-column grid layout with `gutter: 1rem`.
